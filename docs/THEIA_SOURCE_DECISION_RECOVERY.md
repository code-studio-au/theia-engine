## 2026-10-12 creator clarification — Aethyr's reason for the First Sundering

**Status: user-supplied DRAFT of expanded cosmological causation, explicitly not final prose.** Aethyr and Nhal were primordial essences of creation and unmaking, supreme energies **older than space, time, realms and laws**, and categorically more fundamental than the Seven. Their opposing wills reached an immeasurable stalemate. Aethyr conceived a strategy of **imprisoning Nhal rather than destroying it**, so that creation could endure without immediate unmaking. To enact this plan Aethyr **deliberately shattered itself**. The First Sundering brought **both the Physical Realm and the Void** into being at once; Nhal was driven into the newly created Void, Aethyr's shards into the Physical Realm, and **Aethyr's last protective act created the Veil** separating the two realms. The Seven later arose from Aethyr's shards. The exact mechanics of the Veil and final literary wording remain revisable. This clarification **supersedes any earlier implication that the Void pre-existed the First Sundering, that Nhal was merely cast into an already existing Void, or that the Veil formed accidentally**.

## 2026-10-12 user-approved lock — first mortal life and Ascendant's arrival

**Later user decisions superseding earlier unresolved chronology:** The Seven awaken separately; Ascendant spends a meaningful period exploring independently and joins the divine community **only after the first five mortal species already exist**. Guardian and Warrior's bond begins through **philosophical disagreement** about protection and resilience, then grows into mutual respect. Seeker and Shaper meet through **shared magical discovery**, become close collaborators, and discover the principles that make independent consciousness possible. **They choose not to create mortal life because their knowledge is incomplete** (explicitly selected first alternative). Guardian and Warrior take that research further and create **elves first, then orcs**. Shaper next creates **dwarves** after seeing mortals become makers. Judge then creates **humans**, Keeper then creates **halflings**; Seeker remains a scholar and patron rather than creator of one of these six peoples. Ascendant arrives to find five peoples and existing divine relationships, subsequently collaborates with Keeper to create **trolls as the sixth species**, and later strongly advocates the multi-god seventh-people project. His late arrival is not proof of intentional ostracism, inherent evil or sole responsibility for Nhal's corruption.

**Still open:** awakening order of the first six gods, elapsed time and the specific first Seeker–Shaper experiment or Guardian–Warrior encounter. Their thematic relationship origins and the creation sequence are confirmed.

## 2026-10-12 locked correction — the trolls' religious sensitivity

**USER CONFIRMED:** Ascendant's unauthorized alteration to the trolls' design was the **amplification of their inherited connection to divine essence**. His private motive was to encourage the trolls to perceive divinity more strongly and, he hoped, **worship Ascendant over the other gods**. The modification did **not** make them almost unkillable, rapidly regenerating, automatically evolving, immune to illness or death, or mechanically superior in spellcasting. Trolls remained independent mortals; heightened spiritual sensitivity does not dictate belief or guarantee divine communication. Keeper's objection concerned both unauthorized experimentation and the ethical attempt to shape a free people's religious inclinations. The later Korrath devotion to Keeper and Vorrath devotion to Ascendant are culturally and politically developed outcomes, not automatic compulsion. Their schism and exile occurred generations later for mortal religious and political reasons. This **supersedes the unapproved prior draft** of exceptional self-repairing troll magical structures. The joint troll creation, Keeper's stabilization and subtle shared halfling–troll magical resilience remain established.

## 2026-10-12 later creator decision — The Binding and Keeper's deliberate deception

**LOCKED:** The final containment of the First Anchor's damaged homeland is called **The Binding**. The Seven contribute their divine essences to create and maintain **the Shroud**, a barrier within the Physical Realm distinct from Aethyr's Veil. **Keeper knew with certainty before the ritual that all Seven would remain bound to the Shroud, unable to withdraw their essences freely, and deliberately did not disclose that fact.** The other six believed they were making a limited contribution, not accepting indefinite confinement. The betrayal is Keeper's knowingly withheld truth; **The Binding** is the event name. All seven essences and individual consciousnesses survive, allowing the *possible*, not predetermined, later voluntary reunification into a single primordial essence to oppose Nhal. This later decision supersedes older proposed deaths of particular gods and the prior working interpretation of the Great Betrayal as chiefly a secret seizure of shared mortal infrastructure. Exact mechanics and eventual ending remain open.

## 2026-10-12 LOCKED: First Anchor versus modern Unmade Anchors

The user explicitly approved the following distinction: **the unique First Anchor cannot be safely destroyed because it became entangled with the persistent breach in Aethyr's Veil**; it is not materially indestructible. Its destruction could remove the structure still constraining that breach. The First Anchor was made by the seventh people's greatest researchers, whose complete knowledge was lost/isolated after the Second Sundering. **Modern Unmade Anchors are inferior, incomplete derivatives**, built from fragments of that knowledge and drawing upon the **pre-existing damage/connection** rather than making independent permanent Void breaches. They have vulnerable support structures and can be safely disabled or destroyed by players, often through staged disconnection. The progression categories are **Minor Resonators**, **Regional Anchors**, and **Major Anchors**, with escalating danger and complexity; exact numbers and zone implementations are open. Only a coordinated network of sophisticated major Anchors poses a major strain on the Shroud and The Binding. Guardians can guide safe dismantling. This resolves why players can destroy modern Anchors without contradicting the Seven's need to contain the First Anchor. Destroying modern Anchors does not automatically free the Seven or close the original breach.

## 2026-10-12 locked clarification — surviving gates operate continuously

The user explicitly selected **continuous low-capacity operation** for the surviving ancient Passage gates after The Binding, **not** intermittent access during magical alignments. The Shroud weakened and suppressed the ancient network but did not deactivate every gate. Surviving routes permitted individuals, small groups and limited artifacts/supplies to travel through the centuries, while being unable to sustain armies or major supply lines. The Unmade used these routes for covert infiltration of both alliance continents, alongside cells that persisted outside the Shroud. These **physical Passage links** did not restore direct communication with Nhal, and did not bypass containment of the First Anchor. Major PvP gateways may later be restored through progression; conventional shipborne cross-continental incursions remain separate. Exact routes, capacities and whether corrupted seventh-people NPCs also use them remain open.
