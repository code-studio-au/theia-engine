# THEIA — CHARACTER ARCS AND MILESTONES

**Status:** Oth'varen's overarching modern character arc is **creator-approved and locked (14 October 2026)**. The five Corrupted Conclave founder arcs below are **detailed proposals**, not locked canon. Their ancient histories, original roles, names, titles, transformation and loyalties remain established canon. Milestone placement, new encounters and character decisions require later approval.

**Sources:** [Interwoven Chronicle](THEIA_INTERWOVEN_CHRONICLE.md) and [Modern Story and Revelation Timeline](THEIA_MODERN_STORY_REVELATION_TIMELINE.md). This is a player-facing development plan, not a replacement for the historical narrative.

## Oth'varen — locked overarching arc

**Core movement:** *Keeper of Secrets → Restorer of Trust*. Oth'varen is the sixth scholar who resisted Nhal during the First Anchor's activation. His transformed body grants exceptional longevity and power, but he remains mortal, vulnerable and subject to the corruption's persistent pull. He helped found the multiracial Guardians, witnessed the five founders' corruption and survived their first post-Binding reunion. His modern identity remains to be designed.

**Inner conflict:** He believes dangerous knowledge must be contained because he saw trusted scholars conceal and misuse it. Centuries later, Guardian secrecy has also destroyed context, isolated cells and allowed the Unmade to exploit gaps in institutional memory. His challenge is to trust others with knowledge that could be used either to save or endanger Theia.

### Eight milestones — approved direction, not fixed levels

1. **The Unseen Scholar — opening.** The player encounters carefully preserved evidence, Guardian warnings and agents following an unidentified authority. Oth'varen observes their conduct without disclosing his identity.
2. **First Contact — early investigation.** Under a concealed identity, he interprets evidence of a Minor Resonator. His knowledge of six-field stability is unusually deep, and particular configurations visibly disturb him. He shares only what he believes safe.
3. **The Cost of Secrecy — Regional Anchor investigation.** A Guardian cell acts upon incomplete instructions and nearly causes a disaster while attempting to destroy an Anchor. Oth'varen recognises that restrictions he supported contributed to the failure. He begins questioning whether concealment is still protecting people.
4. **The Sixth Scholar — major historical revelation.** Recovered evidence links him to the original First Anchor. He acknowledges his identity, his friendships with the five scholars and his unsuccessful attempt to prevent activation. The player must reassess both his authority and his omissions.
5. **The Voice That Remains — Conclave confrontation.** One of the five exploits the corruption still present within him. His resistance remains an active choice, not an immunity granted by his past success.
6. **The Inversion Hypothesis — Anchor research.** Oth'varen recognises that the network's six-field resonance may be reconfigured to reinforce the Shroud. He advocates investigation rather than reflexive destruction, causing disagreement within the Guardians.
7. **The Trial of Trust — major campaign climax.** A possible inversion requires disclosure of protected knowledge and coordinated action by others. Oth'varen cannot personally control every decision. The player and Guardian allies become essential.
8. **A Different Legacy — resolution.** Regardless of whether an initial inversion is complete or partial, he begins changing the Guardians towards shared responsibility and accountable preservation of knowledge rather than dependence on one immortal witness.

### Relationship pressures — approved direction

- **Vaer'uun, the First Voice:** Oth'varen once respected his leadership; Vaer'uun regards his refusal as betrayal.
- **Thes'var, the Architect:** Their shared technical mastery and opposing understanding of the First Anchor make inversion a central intellectual conflict.
- **Su'vareth, the Veiled Hand:** Her knowledge of his habits and secrecy can be used against Guardian networks.
- **Oma'vira, the Harbinger:** She attacks his conviction that he truly rejected the transformation's invitation.
- **Khor'vun, the Warden:** He sees Oth'varen as a former colleague who abandoned those he once protected.

### Three pivotal player decisions — approved direction

- **Destroy or preserve dangerous evidence.** Early evidence could be weaponised, but may also explain the Anchors.
- **Trust the Sixth Scholar.** His testimony is invaluable, but his concealment and lingering corruption are legitimate grounds for scrutiny.
- **Attempt resonance inversion.** He must entrust other people with knowledge he once would have withheld.

**Constraints:** Do not kill Oth'varen, cleanse his corruption or make him the sole saviour during the launch campaign. His greatest change is learning that secrecy alone cannot prevent another First Anchor. Preserve the full ancient reunion as a separate Memory Scene.

## Corrupted Conclave — proposed parallel character arcs

The five are not interchangeable bosses. Each should pursue Nhal's purpose through an established scholarly talent and a personal interpretation of what the catastrophe meant. Their extraordinary longevity makes them recurring antagonists; they remain vulnerable and not divine. The Conclave formed after The Binding, and Nhal's voice returned only after the Major Anchor network crossed the critical resonance threshold.

### Vaer'uun, the First Voice — authority without certainty

**Historical foundation:** The principal resonance and communication scholar; after the Binding, he claimed the right to interpret Nhal's remembered purpose.

**Core conflict:** His authority was most secure when no one could test it against a fresh message. The return of Nhal's selective whispers vindicates him publicly but may expose differences between his own centuries of doctrine and what Nhal now directs.

**Proposed milestones:**
1. His teachings appear as fragments within apparently unrelated Unmade cells; early followers dispute their meaning.
2. The player uncovers contradictory instructions issued in his name across generations, showing how silence changed doctrine.
3. An inner Conclave directive reveals his attempt to reconcile inherited interpretation with Nhal's newly returned guidance.
4. He authorises increasingly dangerous network operations to prove that his authority remains indispensable.
5. A major confrontation forces a choice between preserving his position and obeying an instruction that undermines it. **His response remains to be decided.**

**Player-facing role:** The voice of ideological certainty, not merely a spellcaster who speaks for Nhal. His weakness is the difference between faith, interpretation and authority.

### Thes'var, the Architect — the unfinished masterpiece

**Historical foundation:** Designed the original Anchor's structures; centuries of experimentation led from Minor Resonators to Regional and Major Anchors.

**Core conflict:** He regards the catastrophe as an incomplete technical achievement. Resonance inversion demonstrates that his methods could serve a purpose opposite to the one he has spent centuries pursuing.

**Proposed milestones:**
1. Players discover a recurring structural signature in Minor Resonators.
2. Regional Anchor research reveals a generational chain of improvements, failures and reused designs.
3. Oth'varen identifies Thes'var's original methods and reluctantly explains their shared history.
4. The Major Anchor network reveals the scale of the Architect's achievement and the consequences of synchronisation.
5. The first credible inversion hypothesis forces the Architect to confront an alternative use of his life's work.
6. A late confrontation centres on control of a major installation rather than a simple attempt to kill its designer. **Whether he attempts to corrupt, seize or destroy an inverted device remains open.**

**Player-facing role:** Principal engineering antagonist and intellectual foil to Oth'varen.

### Su'vareth, the Veiled Hand — the architect of distrust

**Historical foundation:** Controlled restricted records and external relationships; later cultivated hidden networks beyond the Shroud. Her agents orchestrated the Shattered Delegation before Nhal's voice returned.

**Core conflict:** Her success depends on institutions mistrusting one another. The discovery that Covenant and Dominion have both been manipulated threatens her strongest protection: each alliance's certainty that the other is responsible.

**Proposed milestones:**
1. Early missions expose missing artifacts, forged credentials and suspicious shipments without identifying a single organisation.
2. Players discover linked intermediaries operating in both alliances, each unaware of the full network.
3. Contradictions in Shattered Delegation evidence implicate deliberate sabotage; personal grief among alliance leaders complicates disclosure.
4. A Guardian cell is exposed through information that only a trusted insider should have possessed.
5. The Veiled Hand's identity and methods become known, but public proof remains difficult.
6. She responds to threatened exposure by sacrificing cells, planting new evidence or turning investigators against each other. **The precise operation and consequences remain open.**

**Player-facing role:** The antagonist whose actions make truth politically dangerous. She should remain effective without possessing omniscience.

### Oma'vira, the Harbinger — devotion made into certainty

**Historical foundation:** Scholar of consciousness and perception; sustained Unmade devotion and recruitment during the silence.

**Core conflict:** She transformed incomplete memories of Nhal into teachings that successive generations treated as truth. The return of direct whispers threatens to reveal how much she added herself, even as she presents it as fulfilment.

**Proposed milestones:**
1. Players encounter adherents who believe new magical gifts prove divine favour.
2. Recovered teachings show contradictory accounts of the original transformation.
3. A recruitment operation targets people frightened or empowered by the awakening, exploiting genuine needs.
4. A recovered witness or artifact challenges her account of the First Anchor.
5. She attempts to use Oth'varen's surviving corruption as proof that resistance is self-deception.
6. Her followers must confront a divide between loyalty to her doctrine and evidence of manipulation. **The extent of any schism remains undecided.**

**Player-facing role:** The emotional and psychological face of the Unmade, distinct from the Architect's technical ambitions and the First Voice's authority.

### Khor'vun, the Warden — protection turned to imprisonment

**Historical foundation:** Led protective workings and containment for the First Anchor. He now defends its approaches, surviving Unmade strongholds and the network's vital installations.

**Core conflict:** He still defines himself as a protector, but the people and structures he protects perpetuate the threat that devastated his homeland.

**Proposed milestones:**
1. Players encounter unusually disciplined defensive systems guarding an otherwise unremarkable site.
2. A captured stronghold reveals that its safeguards were designed by someone who understood how to protect scholars and civilians.
3. Guardian records identify the same methods as Khor'vun's pre-rupture work.
4. He prevents an operation that might damage the network, forcing players to separate defensive mechanisms from dangerous Anchor workings.
5. A confrontation with Oth'varen reopens their dispute over loyalty, duty and abandonment.
6. A major siege or containment operation tests whether his duty is to preserve the Conclave at any cost. **His ultimate fate remains open.**

**Player-facing role:** A formidable defender whose tragedy lies in confusing loyalty to former colleagues with the protection of life.

## Interlocking milestones — recommended sequence

| Story phase | Oth'varen | Conclave pressure | Guardian and alliance consequences |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Unseen authority | Veiled Hand's infiltrators; Harbinger's local influence | Local conflicts and awakening anomalies |
| Early investigation | Concealed adviser | Architect's Minor Resonators; Warden's defences | First Guardian contacts and evidence preservation |
| Regional Anchor investigation | Questions secrecy | Architect's linked devices; First Voice's inherited directives | Guardian cells disagree over destruction |
| Ancient truth | Sixth Scholar identity | Personal histories of all five emerge | Guardian credibility and trust challenged |
| Shattered Delegation evidence | Helps compare records | Veiled Hand's orchestration exposed in fragments | Alliance leaders face painful evidence |
| Major network and Nhal | Corruption tested | First Voice's authority; Harbinger's doctrine; Architect's coordination | Wider threat recognised without ending faction war |
| Inversion hypothesis | Shares dangerous knowledge | Architect contests purpose; Warden defends installations | Destroy-versus-invert debate |
| Major confrontation | Entrusts others | Multiple founders' objectives intersect | Limited victory, continuing threat |
| Aftermath | Begins changing Guardians | Conclave remains a long-term antagonist | Future Shroud campaign remains open |

## Design guardrails

1. Avoid revealing the five founders' modern titles and ancient identities all at once.
2. Keep Covenant and Dominion hostile; separate investigations may uncover complementary evidence without producing a public alliance.
3. Do not make the Unmade responsible for every legitimate local grievance or every benefit of the awakening.
4. Do not treat a single Major Anchor's destruction or inversion as sufficient to end Nhal's communication or free the Seven.
5. Preserve uncertainty around the method, cost and ultimate consequences of inversion.
6. Do not prematurely assign dungeon or raid deaths to the founders. Their survival or defeat requires specific story decisions.
7. Separate historical canon from proposed encounters and dialogue.

## Next development decisions

The most consequential open questions are **Oth'varen's modern public identity**, the first Guardian contact players meet, which founder appears directly first, and whether the initial inversion proof of concept belongs in the launch campaign or a later chapter. These should be resolved before writing quest scripts.
