# 1 — Cosmic mythology: the complete causal chain

## 1.1 The two primordial powers [CONFIRMED foundation; creator's expanded draft 2026-10-12]

**Aethyr and Nhal were primordial essences, not gods in the later sense.** Their creative and destructive energies were more fundamental than any laws or realms. Before the First Sundering there was no Physical Realm, no Void, no Veil, no space and no time. Thus “eons” describes an immeasurable conflict using a later metaphor, not an actual pre-creation calendar.

Aethyr sought to bring forth and sustain existence. Nhal sought to unmake anything that might be brought forth. Their struggle settled into a stalemate that direct force could not break. Aethyr eventually **conceived a new strategy**: it did not need to annihilate Nhal if it could **imprison Nhal apart from what Aethyr created**. Such a separation required making the very realms and boundary that did not yet exist.

Aethyr deliberately **shattered its own primordial unity**. In the resulting **First Sundering**, the eruption of primordial energy simultaneously brought into being **the Physical Realm** and **the Void**. The force propelled Nhal into the newly formed Void and dispersed Aethyr's shards into the Physical Realm. As its last protective act Aethyr formed **the Veil between the two realms**, so that Nhal could no longer freely unmake physical creation. This was Aethyr's purpose, not an accidental byproduct of a desperate explosion.

The Physical Realm became the place where space, time, matter and the fundamental laws of magic could exist. The Void is a realm of negation rather than a physical demon kingdom. Nhal survived there as a primordial essence but was **confined**. Aethyr's unified consciousness did not survive; from its shattered energies seven equal, distinct divine beings awakened within the Physical Realm, lacking full knowledge of their origin.

**Authorial causation:** stalemate → Aethyr's revelation that confinement could succeed where destruction could not → conscious decision to pay the cost of self-shattering → creation of both realms → displacement of Nhal and Aethyr's shards → deliberate Veil → protected creation → the Seven awakening without memory of the plan. This is the chain that all later histories should preserve.

**Editorial qualification:** This expansion comes from the user's explicit 2026-10-12 **draft clarification**. The causal foundations are intentional creative direction, while the final wording and the Veil's precise mechanics remain open to revision. The earlier confirmed choice that Aethyr shattered itself deliberately is preserved. No older names or discarded light-versus-dark mythology are restored.

## 1.2 The Seven are successors, not servants [CONFIRMED concept; metaphysical specifics WORKING]
The Seven emerged from Aethyr and are independent persons, not puppets or seven full copies. The original primordial struggle is largely unknown to them, while Nhal remembers more. They begin as equals with no high god. Magic was not invented by gods; they learned its laws. Rare acts combine several gods' powers. The seven primordial correspondences considered were continuity/Guardian, understanding/Seeker, will/Warrior, making/Shaper, order/Judge, boundary/Keeper, potential/Ascendant. These are interpretive correspondences, not seven mortal magical disciplines.

The long-term narrative question of *whether recovering Aethyr requires annihilating the individuality of the Seven* was floated as an ethical dilemma, NOT resolved.

## 1.2a The Seven's separate awakenings and Ascendant's arrival [USER-CONFIRMED DIRECTION, 2026-10-12]

The Seven awaken at different times and encounter one another progressively. **Ascendant awakens independently but joins the other gods only after their relationships and mortal creation efforts are already developing.** The user explicitly selected this over the alternative that Ascendant only came into existence after all other gods had created their peoples. His independent period gives him time to discover his powers and establish a philosophy before entering a divine community that already has shared history. His outsider status and envy therefore arise partly from circumstance and interpretation, not necessarily intentional ostracism.

His later desire for a unique legacy makes collaboration with Keeper and advocacy of the seventh-people project more emotionally significant. Keeper's knowledge is needed for the joint troll creation, and Ascendant's unauthorized changes damage their trust. **Not decided:** precise awakening order of the other six, duration of Ascendant's solitude, or whether Ascendant originated the seventh-people proposal. **Now decided:** elves, orcs, dwarves, humans and halflings existed before his arrival. The suggested Guardian–Warrior and Seeker–Shaper early sequence remains a proposal.

## 1.2c Origin of mortal life and six-species chronology [USER LOCKED · 2026-10-12]

The user approved the **Seeker–Shaper discovery-first** model and explicitly chose that they **refrain from creating independent mortal life because their understanding is incomplete**. Their partnership emerges from shared discovery: Seeker investigates fundamental magical principles; Shaper gives them form. They identify how independent consciousness may become possible but cannot fully account for its autonomy and continued stability. Seeker insists on further understanding, while Shaper experiences frustration at the delay.

Guardian and Warrior, already bonded through an initial philosophical disagreement over preservation versus resilience, take that research further and successfully create **the first two mortal peoples: Guardian's elves, then Warrior's orcs**. Their creations express different but equally legitimate visions of independent life. Shaper, inspired by mortals who can themselves create and transform, **then creates dwarves**. Seeker remains investigator/teacher, **not** creator of any of the six original species. Judge **then creates humans** in response to questions of free will, law and social responsibility; Keeper **then creates halflings** with magical stability and resilience in mind.

**Ascendant independently awakened earlier but joins the community only after these five peoples exist.** This is now an explicit chronological lock, superseding the earlier open question about how many peoples had been made before his arrival. He finds established friendships and creations and experiences outsider status, although deliberate exclusion is not established. Ascendant later seeks Keeper's knowledge and they **jointly create trolls as the sixth people**, before Ascendant's unauthorized alterations damage trust. The **seventh people** arise later through several gods' collaborative research, with Ascendant a passionate advocate rather than confirmed sole initiator. Nhal's hidden corruption of that creation remains a confirmed underlying cause.

The **precise awakening order of the first six gods**, their first conversations, and the details of Seeker and Shaper's original discovery remain open as dramatic particulars. The structural order above is confirmed, and must not be undone by an older speculative draft.

## 1.3 The Void and the boundary [working detail]
Void means no conventional space, time, geography, matter, ordinary life or ambient physical magic. It must not become a conventional demon kingdom. **The Veil was deliberately formed by Aethyr during the First Sundering** as the boundary protecting the Physical Realm from the newly created Void. Its existence and purpose are explicit in the creator's 2026-10-12 draft; its exact mechanics remain working, and it is not a new magical school. Ordinary spells do not damage it. Extraordinary workings that probe fundamental reality may create anomalous resonance. Proposed sequence of Nhal's incursion: a working disturbs the boundary; Nhal notices; an exposed mind may receive insight or suggestion; repeated exposure strengthens influence; extraordinary magical structures form persistent anchors; anchors allow limited physical effects and eventually an attempted stable incarnation. Nhal is neither omniscient nor able to dominate any person at will. Its strength lies in providing real but selective knowledge and exploiting preexisting personality flaws.

**OPEN:** precise mechanics, which anchors survive ancient times, limits of mind interference and what a final victory requires.

## 1.4 Why the vanished people mattered [partly CONFIRMED, details WORKING]
The Seven's dispute centred on creating a seventh, extremely magically powerful vanished people. Several gods participated. A working plot gives Shaper and Seeker prominent research roles and Ascendant specialist contributions, with Judge, Guardian and Keeper debating protections, responsibility and boundaries. They were an independent mortal people, NOT Nhal's intrinsically evil creations. Their unusual multi-discipline potential enabled research on the fabric of existence; some working accidentally resonated with the Void and first allowed Nhal to observe and whisper. One imagined first contact was a genuine solution apparently discovered by a scholar; further true discoveries included a subtle dangerous dependency.

The proposed ethnonym **Aethren** was *retired due to similarity with Aethyr*. The lost people have NO confirmed name, location, culture, surviving descendants or fate. Suggestions that surviving descendants are hidden in a magical refuge are **not confirmed**.

## 1.5 The creation dispute and the Divine Schism [CONFIRMED outline, detailed motivations WORKING]
The gods had real disagreements about whether advanced life could be created, whether risks should block inquiry, and who possessed authority to regulate their equal peers. Nhal worked by subtle individual influence, manipulated evidence and the gods' interpretation of one another's actions. The Ascendant had an actual history of unauthorized work during troll creation, making later allegations credible. In a working scenario there is a true dangerous contribution from Ascendant's synthesis research, mixed with **false allegations of intentional corruption/control** planted by Nhal-aligned intermediaries.

Guardian fears for innocents; Seeker demands understanding; Shaper resists condemning creations; Judge insists on accountability and law; Keeper prioritizes containment and keeps dangerous knowledge hidden; Warrior honours transparent judgement and promises; Ascendant fears persecution and conceals research. Mutual suspicion expands into repression, reprisal and war. The mortal magical orders try to remain cooperative until the war. The conflict is complex and shifting, NOT a binary arrangement identical to modern Covenant and Dominion. **Divine combat allegiances have not been decided.**

## 1.6 First and Second Sundering parallel [working full explanation]
Proposed Great Betrayal: Keeper detects a force from outside existence entering through interconnected ancient Aethyr-derived magic; Guardian takes Keeper's warning seriously. They secretly commandeer shared magical infrastructure to reinforce the metaphysical boundary, breaching commitments and threatening innocents, because the others no longer trust them and time is short. Nhal exploits an instability to worsen the cascade. The sealing partially succeeds, expelling much of Nhal's influence but shattering ancient lands and the network of cities/passages; the lost people disappear. This is a **genuine betrayal of others' consent** as well as a protective action, echoing Aethyr's original desperate sacrifice. Other gods are not simply wrong to object. Some call the terminal catastrophe the “Second Sundering” in proposed ancient accounts. The actual identities/actions of the decisive betrayer, gods' fates and the exact sealing sequence remain **WORKING/OPEN**, not endorsed as final canon.

It was proposed that the sealing damaged ambient connections that once supported ancient magical complexity. The network gradually deteriorates, enabling a modern awakening with more wielders, exceptionally gifted people, reactivating enchantments and creatures, plus windows for Nhal. The *awakening is not itself evil*. Most magical phenomena remain natural and potentially positive.

## 1.7 Divine aftermath [WORKING ONLY]
The assistant explored Guardian power committed to protection; Keeper bound to sustained containment; Seeker vanished while investigating; Warrior surviving but wounded by Guardian's broken trust; Shaper entangled with vanished people's infrastructure; Judge withdrawing after legal collapse; Ascendant disappearing under unresolved circumstances. None is a fixed answer on death, survival, resurrection, imprisonment, final divine fate or patron connections. # 3 — Magic as a physical and cultural system

## 3.1 Energy and casting laws [CONFIRMED]
Magic is a primordial foundation of creation, not a resource owned by gods. Intent plus learned technique directs effects. Mortal casting spends from a finite **internal/personal reserve**, shared across disciplines. Aptitude/bloodlines influence capacity and affinity; training enhances capacity and efficiency. Recovery is natural, supported by ambient power and focused meditation. When reserves are depleted, casting normally stops safely. Attempting too-complex effects can overload the mind or lose control. Knowledge, precise discipline mastery and concentration commonly matter more than raw strength. Unstable natural/historical zones change risk and reliability.

## 3.2 Exactly six disciplines [names working, count confirmed]
**Elements & Energy** handles flame, cold, wind, light and other energetic effects. **Matter** manipulates physical materials and transformation; true permanent matter from nothing is ancient/lost. **Life** governs bodily/natural/spiritual operations; resurrection, if possible, needs severe conditions such as available body, consent, timing and extraordinary cost. **Form** governs magical patterns, conditions, wards, relations and structural stability. **Mind** concerns perception, illusion and emotion; mundane free will cannot be simply overridden at low cost. **Passage** concerns spatial transfer and links; long-distance teleportation is largely lost.

Elementary effects use one discipline; advanced two; extraordinary ancient techniques may require multiple. **One wielder must personally know every involved discipline. Normal multi-mage co-casting cannot simply stack participants to bypass expertise.** This does not ban coordinated armies, distinct independently built mechanisms, or exceptionally joint *divine* creative acts.

Enchantments normally have stored/rechargeable reserves; very rare self-sustaining enchantments are a lost craft. Durable creation is difficult and often ancient.

## 3.3 Culture and economics [confirmed choices]
Magic is common in major urban centres, less common in remote settlements. Traditional and magical engineering **compete** rather than one universally replacing the other. Medical traditions differ by people and locality. Magicians are military supports, integrated unit members and sometimes exceedingly powerful rare battle specialists. Warriors remain formidable without magic. Counter-mage traditions include protection/gear, anti-mage tactics and trained specialists. Magical and martial institutions compete for funding, respect and political leverage.

Racial specializations affect access to instructors, distinctive eventually learnable techniques and rare bloodline affinities, but no race is locked out of the disciplines. This is essential for player freedom. Classes, skills, spell lists, cooldowns, resources, stats, numbers and exact balance remain undecided.

# 5 — Chronological reconstruction of mortal history

## 5.1 Divine Age and orders [confirmed historical framework]
Ancient magic was far more sophisticated than today. **Three or four** major independent magical orders (the user's answer was **"Three or four great orders"**, not a fixed four) existed with numerous smaller schools. Orders differentiated by *philosophy + speciality + practical mission*, had several divine influences, shared libraries, research meetings and advanced passages, and openly questioned gods. They were politically independent but interconnected. Unlike the Seven, **most orders remained cooperative until war forced division**.

Surviving masters initially suppressed individual dangerous advanced workings (not whole disciplines). War destroyed institutes, killed wielders/bloodlines, scattered scholars, and caused deliberate restrictions. Some orders hid, were destroyed or fled. Survivors created royal academies, guild/religious schools and independent modern orders. Modern institutions may show direct continuity, fragmentary descent or complete reconstruction. Academic competition is central today.

**Exactly one original great order survives secretly**, but which one, its exact purpose, protection mechanisms, archives and relationship with the Guardians remains unresolved. Earlier assistant-proposed orders of Understanding, Making, Preservation, Horizons were **descriptive placeholders**. Do not finalize Preservation as survivor without approval.

The Guardians, founded during Divine War, are **not automatically the same** as the one surviving older magical order.

## 5.2 The Divine War [historical constraints]
The creator-facing true history may include: escalating restrictions, altered evidence against Ascendant, disagreements over the vanished people, gods splitting over differing duties, mortal cities choosing different divine patrons, damaged magical passages, devastating battles and a final sealing catastrophe. Only the broad schism, divine war, lost people, hidden Nhal influence, ruin and a pivotal betrayal are solidly established; exact wartime sides, first attacker, council wording, chronology and acts of betrayal are not ratified.

The postwar world preserves irreconcilable *myths*, not reliable eyewitness history. A handful of researchers and hidden custodians have fragments of truth. The dying/transforming creator deity's descendants still carry altered divine inheritance.

## 5.3 Recovery and alliance formation
Postwar migrations used now-mostly-failed magical passageways. Ancient surviving dwarf and ancestral troll centres retain genuine fragments of Divine Age infrastructure and scholarship, but authenticity and interpretations vary.

Covenant's origins: human/dwarf pact mutually assured defence and economic cooperation (agricultural plains with mountain engineering). Dwarven autonomy is explicit. The human royal cadet branch governs Covenant, while the human monarch governs a distinct nation. Its multicultural capital is coastal. The exact treaties and names like “Treaty of Crown and Stone” or “War of Broken Roads” were **assistant working lore**.

Dominion's origins: orcs offered halflings protection/refuge during a crisis; partnership evolved around orcish security and halfling guild ingenuity. The Great Clans and guilds retained sovereignty. Later the leadership trials integrated nominations by all four member civilizations. The exact disaster that displaced halflings and founding date are **working**.

The **Aelari/Vaelari** rupture: shared original elven civilization and original capital, then religious/magical schism, city destroyed, new forest and mountain-lake governments, continuing hostility and incompatible histories. Guardian patron for Aelari; Seeker for Vaelari. **Magical responsibility** is the approved principal issue; specifics involving a laboratory accident, civil-war battle, research-complex explosion remain **working**.

The **Korrath/Vorrath** rupture: ancient Keeper-linked highland civilization, religious division centred on the two co-creators, exile of the population later called Vorrath, sacred storm-coast new capital, mutual resentment and different interpretations. Korrath chiefly Keeper; Vorrath chiefly Ascendant. Details of outlawed rituals and who unjustly altered chronicles remain **working**.

The modern war draws strength from territorial/strategic competition, conflicting ancestral claims, repeated reprisals and political legitimacy; it is not solely caused by Nhal. Both alliances are politically and morally complex and roughly balanced. No one side is canonically evil.

# 6 — Shattered Delegation, The Unmade, the Guardians and secret war

## 6.1 Historical event [explicitly CONFIRMED central cause]
Covenant and Dominion, despite entrenched hostility and lack of normal diplomacy, made **an exceptional attempted peace negotiation**. **The Unmade** orchestrated a deliberate attack that destroyed the delegation, prevented peace, caused both alliances to blame the other and reignited/intensified war. **This is not an independent mortal hardliner conspiracy**; such interests might have been exploited but The Unmade planned the event. Exactly how, which delegates died, and who commanded it remain **working**.

Working expansion: infiltrators compromised both delegations' protection; a hidden magical device catastrophically attacked the negotiation site; forged evidence implicated enemy agents to opposite survivors. Alaric and Draven each lost people they valued and carry conflicting assumptions. An ancient Veil-resonance detecting instrument was proposed as a second theft objective; its three-way fragmentation is **NOT confirmed**.

This is the strongest specific historical through-line tying current political anger to Nhal's goals. Nhal exploits but does not wholly cause the otherwise genuine war. The Unmade prefers ongoing war because fragmented power cannot investigate shared reality, military secrecy hides research, and leaders pursue hazardous advantage.

## 6.2 The Guardians [confirmed role; structure working]
Completely secret order founded during the Divine War upon discovery of warning signs of Nhal's involvement. Protective objective: preserve selected true history and dangerous ancient knowledge, monitor possible Veil breaches, stop Nhal returning. May recruit very deeply embedded native Covenant and native Dominion cells. No official joint alliance forces, no open mixed-race neutral quest hubs, no third faction selectable by players. Cross-alliance intelligence belongs to Guardian covert internal channels.

Working internal offices: **Custodians** for archives/policy, **Watchers** for infiltration, **Wardens** for containment/intervention and **Chroniclers** for history. Internal disagreement over secrecy versus intervention is plausible but not finalized. The Guardians are capable of mistakes, and may have failed to prevent the Shattered Delegation.

## 6.3 The Unmade [CONFIRMED name and Nhal allegiance]
User selected **The Unmade**, replacing earlier “The Hollow” working label. Roots in ancient researchers choosing Nhal's influence; infiltrate royalty, commanders, guilds, temples, magical colleges, archaeological expeditions, quartermasters and supply networks in BOTH alliances. Highest leadership knowingly serves Nhal's return. Lesser members might believe they pursue academic privilege or political gain, without recognizing whom they serve.

They are not a playable third alliance. Some agents are native Covenant humans/dwarves/Aelari/Korrath; others native Dominion orcs/halflings/Vaelari/Vorrath. Their infiltration does not create public legal double allegiances.

Working subdivisions: outer circle unwitting followers, inner circle committed occult operatives, core leadership personally committed to Nhal. A hidden planner called **the Architect** was floated as the author of the Shattered Delegation and modern ancient-network activation, still without fixed race/identity.

## 6.4 How players discover the secret
Players enter as ordinary adventurers, not prophesied chosen ones. Local corruption, ambushed patrols, missing research and failed wards generate authentic stories. As players progress, irregularities link to Shattered Delegation and clandestine works. Guardian NPCs can appear under innocent public occupations and later confide limited secrets. **Contact with Guardians as narrative allies does not mean a joinable secondary faction**. Players can sabotage Unmade cells without being able to join Dominion/Covenant opposite sides.

Nhal remains a future primordial revelation. By the level-60 launch climax a player can identify the Unmade and its role in the negotiations, disrupt a regional Void anchor and learn ancient Divine War accounts are false/incomplete, but should not hear the entire true cosmology.

# 7 — Long-form launch story proposals

User-approved structural vision: original mythology should be planned in full for author consistency, but the leveling game must be a big, **classic nonlinear open-world MMORPG**, not an on-rails 1–60 mandatory campaign. Characters may gain experience through exploration, local side narratives, dungeon/group play, professions, war/PvP and selected main chapters. Launch level 60 is a **working target** and the story must provide satisfying victories without revealing the whole saga.

Suggested six thematic acts:
- **I: Roots of the Realm (1–10):** eight distinct beginnings with local conflicts and culturally specific recurring NPCs. Humans deal with garrison corruption, dwarves with malfunctioning deep structures, Aelari with failing forest wards, Korrath with silent ancient wardstones; orcs with clan succession, halflings with an exposed ancient engineering complex, Vaelari with an unsafe experiment, Vorrath with pilgrim/sacred phenomena.
- **II: Restless Frontiers (10–20):** widen into regional settlements and first dungeons; local stories remain intrinsically valuable.
- **III: Gathering War (20–30):** alliance warfare, political pressure, overseas footholds and clues from Shattered Delegation.
- **IV: Echoes of a Forgotten Age (30–40):** ruins awaken, scholars disagree, magic increases, hidden historian/Guardian may guide players without disclosure.
- **V: War Beneath the War (40–50):** Unmade networks identified, selected Guardian agents disclosed, conspiracies around military/academic institutions uncovered.
- **VI: First Reckoning (50–60):** *separate* Covenant and Dominion defences against ancient mechanism/anchor activation. Significant senior Unmade cell exposed or destroyed, faction territory saved, deeper mystery opened.

Campaign working titles **Oaths in Ashes** for Covenant, **The Price of Dominion** for Dominion, **The Severed Chain** for finale/raid. No final quest counts, raid designs or production guarantees. A proposed mortal scholar/political antagonist **Sered Vhal** and the elusive **Architect** appeared in exploratory writing; these are not fully reconciled, and must not be silently merged or canonized.

A set of major continuing historical movements was proposed after launch: rediscover passageways and enter Shrouded Lands, uncover the false divine accusation, understand lost people, face Nhal openly, and eventually confront the cost of restoring an ancient seal. Proposed expansion labels such as *Broken Passage, Inheritance of the Seven, Lost Creation, Reckoning* are NOT final release plans. One working ultimate possibility is mortal civilizations independently contributing distinct infrastructure to maintain the boundary without collapsing into a merged alliance or requiring casual multi-mage spellcasting. **Ultimate ending still OPEN**.

# 8 — Geography, levels, quest regions and world content

# 9 — Overseas incursion warfare and the risk ladder [user-approved direction]

User explicitly introduced **Covenant military outposts, advanced scout posts, research camps and fortified resource hubs on Dominion's continent and Dominion equivalents on Covenant's continent**, with enemy guards, dangerous travel and open-world PvP BEFORE the Shrouded Lands. This **supersedes older descriptions of fully isolated continental populations**. The continent's principal allegiance remains its own; an overseas outpost is an enemy occupation with permanent native-alliance guards, never a neutral cross-faction village.

The user additionally requested that **incursions be geographically and level-wise spread out**, that leveling questlines progress through multiple overseas frontier bands, and that **Guardian and Unmade investigations can use the same incursion framework**. This allows multi-step hidden-war plot threads across both continents without cross-alliance player cooperation.

Working fortification types:
- **Landing garrisons:** permanent defended military arrival, services and safe revival.
- **Forward scout posts:** vulnerable surveillance and intelligence staging.
- **Fortified resource hubs:** mines, quarries, forestry, salvage, transport infrastructure.
- **Research expeditions:** artefact recovery and magical anomalies, susceptible to infiltration by the Unmade.

Working level arc:
- 1–10 secure homeland introductions.
- 10–20 reconnaissance missions and light coastal incursions.
- 20–30 significant small-group outposts, caravans and resource raids.
- 30–40 competing research excavations and ruined structures.
- 40–50 strongholds, counterraids, strategic resources and anti-spy operations.
- 50–60 major overseas staging forts and high-level warfronts.
- Post-unlock Shrouded Lands: bigger, more persistent contested PvP and territorial/guild warfare.

Working examples:
**Broken Coast:** ~24–30, Dominion continent. Covenant fortified landing (proposed name Northwatch Landing), Dominion inland garrison, central ore basin, ruins, research. Covenant reconnaissance/sabotage/supply quests versus Dominion escort/patrol/defence. The location remains Dominion-controlled strategically; Covenant can maintain an overseas foothold without winning the war. Tactical maps in older chat were conceptual, not canonical geography.
**Ashen Reach:** ~24–30, Covenant continent. Dominion coastal expedition and engineering base, Covenant hill fort, ancient quarry, military road, hidden routes and research. Different terrain/quest feel but comparable danger.

Core play principle: a Covenant player sabotaging an enemy supply caravan may encounter Dominion players assigned to protect it; if none arrive, NPC guards/patrols still provide PvE challenge. Neither side's advancement is blocked by the opponent's absence. Friendly arrival/respawn points must resist camping. Lower-level adventurers need protection against level-60 griefing; trial mechanics included PvP-band eligibility/effective level caps, reduced incentives for repeat low-level kills, multiple routes and optional fallback objectives. Precise PvP flagging, combat scaling and control duration are **OPEN**.

Working temporary resource-site state cycle: neutral/contested, Covenant-controlled, Dominion-controlled. Control temporarily provides gathering, supply and strategic benefits but not permanent denial of essential crafting material. Encounters range solo reconnaissance to small-group raids and occasional large events. The Shrouded Lands will feature more lasting territorial warfare. Resource wars should involve lore and logistics rather than anonymous flags.

**Engine implication:** design reusable faction hostility, guard vision/aggro, territorial volumes, NPC patrols, stateful outposts, PvP flagging, contesting, caravan events, respawn safety, anti-grief and persistent server replication. The earlier custom engine discussions concerned a Vulkan/compute prototype and repo work, not yet commitments that these subsystems exist.
